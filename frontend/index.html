How the AI uses policies
  1. Policy file is split into headed sections.
  2. Section embeddings are generated and cached locally.
  3. The query is embedded and compared with policy sections.
  4. A backend evidence gate checks relevance, claim coverage, ambiguity, and conflicts.
  5. Only SUPPORTED evidence may produce a confident policy answer.
