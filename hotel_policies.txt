# CoastalView Hotel Policies

Synthetic academic demo policy source used by the RAG component.

## POL-RES-01 | Reservation Modification Policy
Confirmed reservations may be modified before check-in, subject to room availability. The requested room must be checked for every night. If unavailable, staff may offer alternatives. The assistant must not finalize a change without explicit staff approval.

## POL-VERIFY-01 | Customer Verification Policy
Before displaying or changing reservation details, the customer must match a unique record. If multiple records match the same name, the assistant must request an additional identifier such as reservation number or email and must not guess.

## POL-LOY-01 | Gold Loyalty Benefits
Gold members receive priority support, complimentary room upgrades when available, and late checkout up to 2:00 PM when available.

## POL-ERR-01 | Error & Escalation Policy
If a tool, database, or API returns an error, the assistant must not claim success. The current record should remain unchanged and the issue should be escalated to a staff user.
